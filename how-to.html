HOW RELIC FORGE WORKS

From finished artwork to a launched collection.

Relic Forge is designed so you can make the creative and launch decisions without needing to understand smart-contract internals. This guide explains the normal workflow in plain language. The current public build uses testing-network infrastructure, so always review wallet prompts and permanent actions before signing.

01 · CONNECT AND SAVE

Connect the wallet that owns the project.

Your wallet identifies you as the creator and lets Relic Forge reopen cloud-saved projects from another device. Downloaded backups remain a separate recovery option.

Starting a New Project clears only the project currently open in the browser. It does not delete previously saved projects or downloaded backups.

02 · ARTWORK

Build your layer stack.

Upload an organized folder, create empty trait categories and add artwork later, or make a category from loose files. You can rename, reorder, add, and remove categories and traits throughout the build.

Relic Forge shows every uploaded trait so you can visually confirm the complete artwork set before continuing. Individual trait files that are too large for the onchain format are rejected during upload instead of failing later.

Optional full-canvas 1/1 artwork can occupy its own collection slot and use its own token name, description, and custom attributes.

03 · RARITY & METADATA

Choose how often each trait appears.

Use rarity tiers, percentages, exact amounts, or equal distribution. Auto Fill keeps any values you entered yourself and distributes only the amount that is still unassigned.

You can also hide selected “None” traits, entire categories, or individual trait values from token attributes without preventing the artwork from rendering.

04 · RULES

Tell Relic Forge what can appear together.

Create compatibility rules before generation—for example, a specific hat may require a certain head type, or two traits may never be allowed together.

Relic Forge checks the rule set for conflicts and shows examples so you can correct impossible combinations before building the final collection.

05 · CURATE & PREVIEW

Generate, inspect, and fine-tune.

Preview the collection, inspect rarity results, look for duplicates, and manually lock specific traits to specific token numbers when desired.

The same generation seed produces the same result. You can keep a chosen seed for repeatability or create a new one to generate another arrangement.

06 · CREATE THE COLLECTION

Turn the finished project into an onchain collection.

Relic Forge stores the final artwork and collection plan, creates the NFT collection, verifies the finished data, and permanently locks the artwork when you approve the final step.

You choose whether the creator covers the Relic Forge platform fee up front or collectors cover the per-mint platform fee as they mint.

Important: creating and locking the collection does not automatically open minting. New collections begin with minting paused so you can review the deployed result before selecting Resume Mint.

07 · MINT STAGES

Control who can mint, when, and for how much.

Create one or more mint stages. Each stage can have its own price, opening time, closing time, supply limit, wallet limit, and access type.

You can pause the entire mint without changing any stage settings, or pause just one stage while leaving the others available. Launched collections can also add new stages later while creator control remains active.

08 · MINT PAGE & UPCOMING MINTS

Publish the page collectors will use.

Add a collection image and banner, choose which open and approved-wallet stages should appear on the public mint page, and optionally show the collection in Upcoming Mints.

When a selected stage has a scheduled opening time, the public page uses that time for its countdown. You can remove the project from Upcoming Mints at any time without changing the NFT collection itself.

09 · REVEAL

Choose Forge Reveal or Reveal Later.

Forge Reveal starts the verified-randomness process automatically after each mint batch. Once the randomness arrives, the reveal is completed and those NFTs receive their final artwork combinations.

Reveal Later keeps newly minted NFTs hidden until you decide to reveal the pending group. You start the reveal from the Creator Dashboard, wait for verified randomness, and then complete the reveal. Large groups can be completed across more than one transaction.

You can change the reveal choice for future mints. NFTs already waiting under Reveal Later stay in that reveal queue until they are completed.

10 · CREATOR DASHBOARD

Manage the collection after launch.

The Creator Dashboard exposes the collection controls that remain available after launch: pause/resume minting, add or edit mint stages, creator mint to another wallet, change the future reveal choice, reveal pending NFTs, manage reveal funding, update payout and royalty wallets, send creator earnings, and review permanent-control status.

Display settings can be changed only before artwork and metadata are permanently locked. Relic Forge shows those settings as locked afterward so it is clear why they can no longer be changed.

11 · COLLABORATION

Share project editing, not wallet authority.

Add collaborator wallets and choose which parts of the editable Studio project they may work on: artwork, rarity, rules, preview, launch setup, or mint-page presentation.

Collaborators use their own wallets. They do not receive the creator’s private key and cannot impersonate the creator for onchain collection-control transactions.

12 · PERMANENT ACTIONS

Relic Forge separates “locked artwork” from “surrendered control.”

Locking artwork & metadata permanently freezes the collection’s stored visual content and collection plan.

Permanently Surrender Creator Control is a separate, irreversible action. Once used, you can no longer pause minting, edit mint stages, change future reveal behavior, change payout/royalty settings, or creator-mint. Relic Forge keeps this action in a dedicated warning area and requires explicit confirmation.
