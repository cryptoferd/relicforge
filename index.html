FROM IDEA TO COLLECTION

A creative process, not a coding project.

Relic Forge brings the technical machinery together in a visual Studio. You make the creative decisions; the platform prepares the onchain collection.

01

Bring your artwork

Upload layered artwork, add individual traits, or create standalone 1/1 pieces. Organize and refine everything as you go.

02

Shape the collection

Set rarity using percentages or exact amounts. Add trait rules, curate specific tokens, and preview the complete result.

03

Configure your launch

Choose your network, mint prices, sale stages, approved wallets, and reveal experience. Review the details before deployment.

04

Forge & manage

Deploy your collection, publish its mint page, and manage minting and reveal from your Creator Dashboard.

BUILT FOR CREATORS

Simple to use.
Precise when it matters.

Start with the artwork you have. Relic Forge gives you room to refine it, without forcing you into a rigid creation process.

Build your way. Upload folders, create categories manually, add new traits later, or import an existing collection recipe.

Control every outcome. Combine weighted rarity, exact trait quantities, compatibility rules, and hand-curated tokens.

Choose your reveal. Mint hidden NFTs and reveal later, or use Forge Reveal to reserve tokens before verified randomness assigns their artwork.

Keep the important things onchain. Collection artwork, metadata, and recipe data are stored in immutable onchain data contracts when sealed. Optional display caches are separate from the canonical artwork.

PERMANENCE IS THE POINT

Created with intention.
Preserved in code.

Your collection is more than an image on a marketplace. Relic Forge is designed around onchain artwork, verifiable collection data, and creator-controlled launch decisions. Once sealed, the artwork and metadata cannot be edited through the collection's creator controls.
